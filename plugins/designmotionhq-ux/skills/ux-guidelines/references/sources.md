# Sources

Every rule in this skill is distilled from the public videos of [@designmotionhq](https://www.instagram.com/designmotionhq/) and their free written breakdowns at [designmotionhq.com/patterns](https://www.designmotionhq.com/patterns). The wording here is our own; the ideas are theirs. Their videos and frames are not included. Their paid plugin, UX Engine, is a separate product and was not used.

Colours shown in the videos are deliberately left out: the rules describe relationships (contrast, roles, steps), so they apply to any palette.
